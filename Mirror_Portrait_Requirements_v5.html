Mirror Portrait · complete requirements · v5

Every requirement, what it should feel like when it works, and how it fails

Sixty-seven requirements in nine groups. Each carries a driver, the relations it must preserve, the outcome the artist should experience, and the named failure it exists to prevent. Diagrams sit with the group they govern. Fifty-seven dynamics are carried forward from the Combined Production Specification v2; ten are added by the geometric analysis and marked G.

1 · Purpose and outcomes

The tool solves an inverse problem and hands you something you can execute.

Given an observed mirror portrait, find a physically consistent apparatus that reproduces its composition, and emit instructions you can carry out with a real mirror and a real phone. The artwork is a representational circuit: direct embodied front self, mirror reflected rear self, phone digitally framed self, outer world. The value is in those states holding together in one image.

Four products, which with two diagnostics are the six output modes: FINAL_CAMERA, COMPOSITION_OVERLAY, REFERENCE_RENDER, STAGING_PRESCRIPTION, plus MASK and FULL_SENSOR.

2 · Geometry requirements 10 requirements

The physics has to be right before the interface can be judged.

These ten come from analysing the apparatus rather than the interface. Each is a closed-form result or a measured fact, and each corresponds to a defect found in the current build or a gap in the existing catalogue.

idrequirementwanted outcomefailure mode
G01Reflection transform applies to skinned meshes
Reflected skinned meshes must use detached bind mode so the Householder matrix reaches the vertices.
test · ACC-REF-01		The reflection appears inside the mirror and the direct body renders once.Attached bind cancels the mesh world matrix, so the reflected clone renders coincident with the direct body at partial opacity.
G02Semantic bones resolve through the loader
Bone lookup uses the original glTF name, preserved at userData.name, not the sanitised node name.
test · ACC-RIG-01		All 24 semantic joints drive the mesh; boot fails loudly if any does not.GLTFLoader strips dots, so 47 of 54 bones silently fail to resolve and every limb stays in rest pose.
G03Vertical field of view is derived correctly
vfov = 2·atan((H/W)·tan(hfov/2)). Never hfov divided by aspect.
test · ACC-CAM-02		The preview frames the same content as the export.Dividing degrees by aspect gives 132.8° where 86.1° is correct on a portrait phone.
G04Aperture visibility is a band, not a boolean
Reflection height is z_r = (p·z_c + c·z_p)/(c+p). Required aperture height is stature·c/(c+p) and required sill is p·z_c/(c+p).
test · ACC-REF-02		The artist sees which body parts survive the mirror and what mounting height would fix it.A boolean per point tells the artist nothing about what to change.
G05Lateral offset is a hard constraint
The phone must sit at least (1 + a/2m) head radii off the face axis or the direct head eclipses its own reflection.
test · ACC-FEA-01		The reflection is always visible; the tool warns before it is not.Vertical offset alone cannot separate them: in the vertical section face and mirror overlap by 27.4°.
G06Reach is handed
Reach is measured shoulder to camera, not face to camera, and crossing the body costs 6 to 8 cm.
test · ACC-FEA-01		Which hand holds the phone constrains which side the mirror can be on, and the tool says so.Treating arm extension as a free scalar admits configurations no human can hold.
G07The reflected phone is a calibration target
Its image position is the mirror-normal vanishing point, its apparent roll is the mirror's own, and it sits at exactly 2c.
test · ACC-PQ-01		Four of the mirror's six degrees of freedom are measured from one small region in every frame.Ignoring it forces mirror pose to be inferred from soft body landmarks instead.
G08Scale ratio is the trusted quantity
R = |C−F′|/|C−F| is a ratio of image sizes, so focal length cancels. Absolute distances inherit the full HFOV error.
test · ACC-EPI-01		Calibration-free numbers render solid; calibration-dependent ones render hollow with a band.One measured phone dimension portrayed as making every scene distance certain.
G09Recursion is synthesis, declared as such
Scale per level is W_s/(4c·tan(hfov/2)), about 0.016 at 70°. Level three does not exist optically.
test · —		The recursion mode is labelled as a construction on a solved apparatus.Presenting the Print Gallery render as an optical prediction of the photograph.
G10Reference masks are machine-read
Hue carries the semantic part, value carries reflection state, and the mirror polygon partitions direct from reflected.
test · ACC-MSK-01		Occupancy, centroids, bounding boxes and medial axes extract automatically; adding a tenth reference takes minutes.Hand-measuring landmarks, and a value convention applied inconsistently so classification by value fails.

5 · Mirror And Reflection 7 requirements

The mirror is a finite aperture in a real wall, and the reflection obeys it.

Reflected content is clipped hard at the aperture. Which body parts appear is a computed band with per-part percentages, not a boolean. Overlap between the direct body and the reflection is declared intent, not a fault.
